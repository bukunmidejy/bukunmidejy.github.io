About Zenvoy

What is Zenvoy Mobility?

Zenvoy is an on-demand, chauffeur-driven mobility service in Lagos. You request a vehicle and a professional chauffeur for a period of time — from two hours to a full day — and we handle the car, the chauffeur and the coordination for that period.

How is Zenvoy different from ride-hailing?

Ride-hailing is built around a trip. Zenvoy is built around your time. Your vehicle and chauffeur are assigned to you for the hours you book and wait between stops, so a day with five addresses in it is one booking instead of five separate arrangements.

Which areas do you cover?

Lagos. Journeys that go outside Lagos can sometimes be arranged and are quoted separately, so ask us before you plan around one.

Do you own the vehicles?

Some of the vehicles we use are our own and others come from vetted vehicle partners, depending on what you have requested and what is available. Either way your booking is with Zenvoy: we arrange the vehicle, brief the chauffeur and stay your point of contact.

Booking

Can I request a car for today?

Yes. Requests can be for now, later the same day, or a date ahead. Send one and we'll come back with what's available. Zenvoy isn't an instant-dispatch service — every request is checked against a real vehicle and a real chauffeur before we confirm. More notice helps for full days, airport runs at peak times and event days.

Can I get a car for several hours?

Yes. That is the core of the service. The minimum is two hours, and four, eight and twelve-hour bookings are common. For several days or a recurring arrangement, tell us the pattern and we will quote it.

Can I make multiple stops?

Yes. Within your booked hours and the agreed area, the vehicle stays with you and moves as your day does. Send a rough itinerary with your request so the chauffeur can plan it with you.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes, at Murtala Muhammed International and the domestic terminals. Send the flight number if you have it, so the timing is planned around the flight.

How do I book, and when is it confirmed?

Send a request from the booking page. You get a reference number immediately, but that is a request, not a confirmed booking: Zenvoy then checks vehicle and chauffeur availability and comes back with the final price. The booking is confirmed only once you accept it.

How do I pay?

Nothing is charged when you send a request. We confirm the price and how to settle it when we confirm your booking, before the car moves. There is no meter and no surge pricing.

Vehicles and chauffeurs

What vehicles are available?

An electric vehicle at published hourly rates, plus executive and premium vehicles quoted per booking. Larger vehicles for groups can sometimes be arranged. Availability varies by day, so the vehicle is confirmed when your booking is confirmed.

Can you provide a specific vehicle?

Tell us your preference and we will work to it, but we only confirm a vehicle once we know it is free for your time. Confirming something we can actually deliver beats promising a model and substituting it on the day.

Are chauffeurs included?

Yes. Every Zenvoy vehicle comes with a chauffeur. We do not hire vehicles out for self-drive.

Is fuel or charging included?

For the electric vehicle, charging for normal movement within Lagos during your booked hours is included in the published rate. For other vehicles, what the rate covers is set out in your quote before you confirm. Tolls, parking and travel outside Lagos are quoted separately.

Corporate

Can businesses open corporate accounts?

Yes. Zenvoy Corporate is built for companies that move executives, employees, clients and visiting guests. You name the people authorised to request transportation, we agree rates and a process, and requests are placed against the account. See Zenvoy Corporate.

Do corporate customers get credit?

Not automatically. Consolidated invoicing and any credit terms are available to eligible companies, subject to approval, and are agreed in advance as part of setting up the account.

Changes and cancellations

What happens if I need more time?

Message us as soon as you know. If the vehicle is free, additional hours are charged at the applicable rate and confirmed with you before they are added. If it has another booking after yours, we will tell you early so you can plan.

What happens if I cancel?

Tell us as early as you can. Cancellation terms are confirmed with your booking; late cancellations may be charged, because the vehicle and chauffeur were held for you.

What if my flight is delayed or my day runs late?

Send us a message. Delays are part of this business and we plan for them. Where the vehicle is free we adjust the booking, and where it is not we say so quickly.
