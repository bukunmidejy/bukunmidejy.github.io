About Zenvoy

What is Zenvoy Mobility?

Zenvoy is a Lagos-based, pre-booked chauffeur service. You book a vehicle and a professional chauffeur for a defined period of time — from two hours to a full day — and we handle the vehicle, the chauffeur and the coordination for that period.

How is Zenvoy different from Uber, Bolt or inDrive?

Those services answer the question “where are you going?” and price a single trip. Zenvoy answers “how long do you need the car?” Your vehicle and chauffeur are reserved in advance and stay with you for the hours you booked, waiting between stops rather than leaving after each drop-off.

Which areas do you cover?

Lagos. Bookings that involve travel outside Lagos can sometimes be arranged, but they are quoted separately — ask us before you plan around it.

Do you own the vehicles?

Some of the vehicles we use are our own and others come from vetted vehicle partners, depending on what you have booked and what is available. Either way, the booking is with Zenvoy: we arrange the vehicle, brief the chauffeur and remain your point of contact.

Booking

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can. A day or two of notice is comfortable for most bookings, and more helps for full days, events and busy travel periods. Same-day requests are still worth sending — we will tell you straight away whether we can cover them.

Can I book a car for several hours?

Yes — that is the core of the service. The minimum booking is two hours, and four, eight and twelve-hour bookings are common. For several days or a recurring arrangement, tell us the pattern and we will quote it.

Can I make multiple stops?

Yes. Within your booked hours and the agreed area, the vehicle stays with you and moves as your day does. Send us a rough itinerary when you book so the chauffeur can plan the day with you.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Pickups and drop-offs at Murtala Muhammed International and the domestic terminals are pre-booked. Send the flight number if you have it, so timing can be planned around the flight.

How do I pay?

We confirm the rate and how to settle it when we confirm your booking, before the day. There is no meter and no surge pricing.

Vehicles and chauffeurs

What vehicles are available?

An electric vehicle at published hourly rates, plus executive and premium vehicles quoted per booking. Larger vehicles for groups can sometimes be arranged. Availability varies, so the vehicle is confirmed when your booking is confirmed.

Can you provide a specific vehicle?

You can tell us your preference and we will do our best to meet it, but we only confirm a vehicle once we know it is available for your date. We would rather confirm something we can deliver than promise a particular model and substitute it on the day.

Are chauffeurs included?

Yes. Every Zenvoy booking is chauffeur-driven. We do not hire vehicles out for self-drive.

Is fuel or charging included?

For the electric vehicle, charging for normal movement within Lagos during your booked hours is included in the published rate. For other vehicles, what the rate covers is set out in your quote before you confirm. Tolls, parking and travel outside Lagos are quoted separately.

Corporate

Can businesses open corporate accounts?

Yes. Zenvoy Corporate is built for companies that move executives, employees, clients and visiting guests. You nominate the people authorised to book, we agree rates and a booking process, and bookings are placed against the account. See Zenvoy Corporate.

Do corporate customers get credit?

Not automatically. Consolidated invoicing and any credit terms are available to eligible companies, subject to approval, and are agreed in advance as part of setting up the account.

Changes and cancellations

What happens if I need more time?

Message us as soon as you know. If the vehicle is free, additional hours are charged at the applicable rate and confirmed with you before they are added. If the vehicle has another booking after yours, we will tell you early rather than leaving you stranded.

What happens if I cancel?

Tell us as early as you can. Cancellation terms are confirmed with your booking; late cancellations may be charged, because the vehicle and chauffeur were held for you.

What if my flight is delayed or my day runs late?

Send us a message. Delays happen constantly in this business and we plan for them. Where the vehicle is available, we adjust the booking; where it is not, we will say so quickly.
