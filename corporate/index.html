What it covers

The movements your business already has.

Most companies do not need a fleet. They need a car and a chauffeur to be in the right place, at the right time, for a few hours — reliably, and without someone chasing it.

That is what a Zenvoy Corporate account is for.

  • Executives moving between meetings across Lagos
  • Employees travelling for business
  • Clients you are hosting or sending home
  • Visiting executives from other offices or countries
  • Airport pickups and drop-offs
  • Conferences, dinners and company events
  • Recurring weekly transportation

Setting up

How an account works.

There is no portal to log into and no contract to sign before you can talk to us. Setting up an account is a conversation.

  1. Tell us how your company moves

    A short conversation about who travels, how often, and the kind of transportation you need.

  2. We agree rates and process

    Rates for the vehicles you are likely to use, and how bookings should reach us.

  3. You nominate authorised bookers

    The people in your organisation — assistants, office managers, operations — who can place bookings on the account.

  4. Bookings are placed and confirmed

    Each booking is confirmed with the vehicle, chauffeur and rate before the day.

  5. You are invoiced

    Consolidated invoicing is available to approved accounts. Credit terms, where offered, are agreed in advance.

Straight answers

What we will and will not promise.

We are an early-stage company operating in Lagos. Being clear about this is part of being a useful partner.

  • Availability is confirmed booking by booking. We do not claim unlimited capacity, and we will tell you early if we cannot cover a date.
  • Vehicles are confirmed, not guaranteed by model. We source from our own supply and vetted vehicle partners, and we confirm the vehicle with the booking.
  • Credit is subject to approval. Consolidated invoicing and any credit terms are agreed in advance with eligible companies, not granted automatically.
  • Support runs with your bookings. You have a contact while your booking is running. We do not run a 24-hour dispatch desk.
