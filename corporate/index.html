What it covers

The movements your business already has.

Most companies do not need a fleet. They need a car and a chauffeur in the right place, at the right time, for a few hours, without someone chasing it between calls.

That is what a Zenvoy Corporate account is for.

  • Executives moving between meetings across Lagos
  • Employees travelling for business
  • Clients you are hosting or sending home
  • Visiting executives from other offices or countries
  • Airport pickups and drop-offs
  • Conferences, dinners and company events
  • Recurring weekly transportation

Setting up

How an account works.

There is no portal to log into and no contract to sign before you can talk to us. Setting up an account is a conversation.

  1. Tell us how your company moves

    A short conversation about who travels, how often, and the kind of transportation you need.

  2. We agree rates and process

    Rates for the vehicles you are likely to use, and how requests should reach us.

  3. You name your bookers

    The people in your organisation — assistants, office managers, operations — who can request transportation on the account.

  4. Requests are made and confirmed

    Each one is confirmed with the vehicle, chauffeur, timing and price before the car moves.

  5. You are invoiced

    Consolidated invoicing is available to approved accounts. Credit terms, where offered, are agreed in advance.

How we operate

Built around reliability.

Every request is handled through Zenvoy. We confirm availability, arrange the vehicle and the chauffeur, and stay your point of contact for the whole service.

Two things we say plainly, because they affect how you plan: we confirm a vehicle for each booking instead of guaranteeing a particular model, and corporate credit is agreed in advance with approved companies rather than granted automatically.

  • Availability is confirmed before a booking is set, not assumed.
  • Chauffeurs are assigned and briefed by Zenvoy for each service.
  • One point of contact, from the request through to drop-off.
  • Booking details in writing: vehicle, chauffeur, timing and price.
  • Vehicle options drawn from Zenvoy and vetted vehicle partners.
  • Pricing agreed before the service runs. No meter, no surge.
