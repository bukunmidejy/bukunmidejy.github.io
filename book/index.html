What happens next

Request, then confirm.

Zenvoy does not run automatic dispatch. Every request is checked against a real vehicle and a real chauffeur before it becomes a booking.

  1. You send the request

    It reaches Zenvoy with a reference number attached.

  2. We check availability

    A real vehicle and a real chauffeur, for your date and hours.

  3. We confirm the price

    Published rates for the electric; a quote for everything else.

  4. Your booking is confirmed

    Only once you have accepted. Nothing is charged before that.
