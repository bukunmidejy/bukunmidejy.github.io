What Zenvoy is

You don’t always need another ride. Sometimes you need a car for the next few hours.

Some days are one trip. Most are not. Three meetings, a client to drop at their hotel, a flight at six — and a car that leaves after every stop means finding transport again at every address.

Zenvoy is the other way round. You get a vehicle and a professional chauffeur for the hours you need, and the car waits between stops. Finding the car, assigning the chauffeur and keeping the day on track is our job, not yours.

Common questions

Before you request a car.

Can I request a car for today?

Yes. You can request one now, later today, or for a date ahead. Send the request and we will tell you straight away what is available. Longer bookings, airport runs at peak times and event days fill up faster, so more notice helps.

How is Zenvoy different from ride-hailing?

Ride-hailing is built around a trip. Zenvoy is built around your time. You get a vehicle and a professional chauffeur for a set period: two hours, a half day, a full day. The car stays with you for that period instead of leaving after each drop-off.

What is the minimum?

Two hours. From there you can request four, eight or twelve hours, or ask us to quote something longer.

Are chauffeurs included in the price?

Yes. Every Zenvoy vehicle comes with a chauffeur. We do not hire vehicles out for self-drive.
