The difference

You don’t always need another ride. Sometimes you need a car for the next few hours.

Ride-hailing is built around single trips. You order a car, you take the trip, the car leaves. When your day involves three meetings, a client to drop off and a flight at six, that means starting again every time — new car, new driver, new wait.

Zenvoy works differently. You book a vehicle and a professional chauffeur for a block of time. Between stops, the car waits for you. We arrange the vehicle, brief the chauffeur and stay reachable while the booking runs.

Common questions

Before you book.

How is Zenvoy different from Uber or Bolt?

Ride-hailing is built around single trips ordered on the spot. Zenvoy is booked ahead: you reserve a vehicle and a professional chauffeur for a set period — two hours, a half day, a full day — and the car stays with you for that time.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can. A day or two ahead is comfortable for most bookings, and more notice helps for full days, events and airport runs at busy times. Same-day requests are worth sending — we will tell you straight away whether we can cover it.

What is the minimum booking?

Two hours. From there you can book four, eight or twelve hours, or ask us to quote for a longer arrangement.

Are chauffeurs included in the price?

Yes. Every Zenvoy booking is chauffeur-driven. We do not hire vehicles out for self-drive.
