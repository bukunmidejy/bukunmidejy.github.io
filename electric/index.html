The detail

What the rate covers.

  • The electric vehicle for the hours you book.
  • A professional chauffeur, briefed on your booking.
  • Charging for normal movement around Lagos within those hours.
  • One point of contact at Zenvoy while the booking runs.

Quoted separately

  • Time beyond the hours you booked, confirmed with you before it is added.
  • Travel outside Lagos, or anything unusually long.
  • Tolls and parking charges where they apply.

Worth knowing

Booked ahead, one vehicle.

We run a small operation. The electric vehicle is booked on a first-come basis, so dates and long bookings go early — particularly weekends and event season.

Tell us the date and the hours as soon as you know them. If the electric is already taken, we will say so and offer an executive or premium alternative rather than leave you waiting.
